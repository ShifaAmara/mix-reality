A macaron is a biscuit made with egg whites , powdered sugar , refined sugar , almond flour , and food coloring . Macarons are small, round, multicolored biscuits, crispy on the outside, but with a soft creamy center that melts in the mouth.

There is some debate in the use of the terms macaron and macaroon , because there is a typical Italian coconut macaron which is quite different from the typical French macaron ( macaron ).

In English, some bakers have adopted the French spelling, macaron , for this pastry, to distinguish it from the macaroon . Stanford University linguistics professor Daniel Jurafsky explains that both foods share a common history with macaroni ( Italian : maccheroni ). Jurafsky notes that French words ending in "-on" that entered English in the 16th and 17th centuries typically underwent spelling changes to end in "-oon," such as balloon ( ballon ), cartoon (from French : carton ), and platoon ( peloton ). Consequently, many bakeries continue to use the term "makaroon".

Although only recently popular in Indonesia, the history of the macaron can be traced back to the 16th century. At that time, the Italian Queen Catherine de' Medici popularized the initial version in France. According to tradition, the macaron was introduced to France by Queen Catherine de' Medici 's Italian chef during the Renaissance .

In 1792, during the French Revolution, two nuns who became known as the 'Macaron Sisters' sold the sweet treats to survive.

The macaron recipe has evolved since its inception. In 1830, in Paris, bakers began joining two cookie balls together, filling them with jam or other fillings—this is the version we know today.

Since the 19th century, Parisian macarons have been served with a ganache , buttercream , or jam filling sandwiched between two biscuits, giving them a similarity to a layered biscuit. They are characterized by a smooth, slightly curved top, a rough, rounded center, and a flat base. They are slightly moist and melt in the mouth and can be varied in flavors, ranging from traditional ( raspberry , chocolate ) to more unusual flavors ( foie gras , matcha ). This also means that macarons can be found in a variety of colors to suit their flavors. (source: https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makaron)
