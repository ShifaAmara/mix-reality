A donut is a fried pastry made from a mixture of wheat flour , granulated sugar , egg yolks , yeast, and butter . The most common donut is a ring- shaped doughnut with a hole in the center, usually sprinkled with sprinkles or powdered sugar. Round donuts are filled with sweet fillings, such as jam , jelly , cream , chocolate, and custard .

Donuts are completely different from bagels —from the ingredients, the technique used to serving them, and even the toppings used, even though they both have almost the same shape.
